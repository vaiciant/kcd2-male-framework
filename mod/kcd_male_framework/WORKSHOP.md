# KCD2 Male Framework

**Play Henry's story with another face.**

Give Henry any man's face of Kingdom Come: Deliverance II with one console command, and get his own face back whenever you like. He keeps his story, his skills and his things.

## What you get

- **74 faces.** Every common man's face of the game, young and old, and a man of the story.
- **Head and body.** Each face comes with its own body, so the arms and the skin match.
- **First person** looks clean: you never see the inside of the face or the beard.
- **A barber that fits.** Ask any barber for a haircut and pick the hair and the beard in the talk, among those that fit the face, and see each one before you take it. It costs the usual 50 groschen, or 5 with Realish Economy. With his own face, Henry has the game's barber as always.
- **The game's scenes** keep his face where Henry is wet, wounded or bandaged, and in the pillory.
- **Saving and loading** keep the face.

## How to play

Open the console with the key under Esc, and type:

- `ml_mf_looks` to see every face, and which characters of the game wear it.
- `ml_mf_look man_czech_2` to give Henry that face, or `ml_mf_random` for one at random.
- `ml_mf_henry` to give him his own face back.
- `ml_mf_start man_romani_4` in the main menu, before New Game, to start the new game with that face. It goes on once Henry rides out after the prologue with Godwin.

The screen goes dark for a few seconds while he changes. Type the commands in the game, not in the inventory or the map: there, the change waits until you close them.

## Good to know

- It changes the barber's talk and the men's head models, so mods that change those can clash with it.
- While Henry is a woman, with KCD2 Female Framework, `ml_mf_look` gives him a man's face straight away: there is no need to go back to Henry first. Use KCD2 Female Framework 0.4.0 or later.

## Removing it

Type `ml_mf_uninstall` in the console, save, and then remove the mod. Henry keeps his own face.

## Roadmap to 1.0.0

Plans, not promises.

## Installing

Subscribe, or download it from Nexus Mods and put its folder in the game's `Mods` folder. That is all: no launch options and no tools.

## For mod makers

Any mod can use it: one line of Lua gives Henry another face. It is free for any mod, closed or open, as long as you credit it where people can see it. The source and the guide for mod makers are on [GitHub](https://github.com/vaiciant/kcd2-male-framework).

*Works on its own or with KCD2 Female Framework. Required by KCD2 Life.*
